Application Procedures

How to Apply

Follow our structured 4-step recruitment process to submit your qualifications for consideration.

1

Choose a Position

Browse current vacancies, review duty statements, and confirm alignment with your background.

2

Prepare Documents

Assemble your application letter, notarized PDS, and complete supporting credentials.

3

Submit Application

Email your complete application dossier and supporting credentials directly to our HR recruitment inbox.

4

Wait for Our Review

Our recruitment committee screens credentials and contacts shortlisted candidates for interview.

Required Documents

  1. 1. Application Letter addressed to the Executive Committee, stating the specific position applied for.
  2. 2. Duly notarized Personal Data Sheet (PDS) with recent passport-sized picture (CS Form 212, Revised 2017).
  3. 3. Certificate of Employment from previous employers or organizations, detailing relevant duties.
  4. 4. Certificate of Trainings Attended in community development, project management, or specialized fields.
  5. 5. Certified True Copy of Transcript of Records (TOR) from an accredited collegiate institution.
  6. 6. Certified True Copy of PRC License or Civil Service Eligibility (if applicable to the post).
NOTE: Applications with incomplete documents will not be processed.

Prefer Email?

You can also send your complete application by email. Please compress your scanned documentary requirements into a single PDF or ZIP archive.

Our Culture & Values

Why Work With Us

Contribute your skills toward meaningful grassroots impact across Butuan City and the Caraga Region.

Purpose-Driven Work

Serve artisanal fisherfolk, coastal communities, and youth across Caraga Region through participatory social initiatives.
