Who We Are

About Friendship Goals Society Caraga-Butuan Inc.

A grassroots non-governmental organization working at the intersection of community welfare, coastal care, youth leadership, and regional civil society governance in Butuan City and the Caraga Region.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower grassroots communities, artisanal fisherfolk, and youth in Butuan City and Caraga through participatory social services, skills-based livelihood opportunities, active environmental stewardship, and dedicated civic representation in regional development policy-making.

🔭

Our Vision

A resilient, self-sustaining Caraga Region where coastal communities thrive, youth actively resist substance abuse through positive leadership, and civil society voices collaboratively guide inclusive regional growth.

🌟

Our Core Values

  • Grassroots Solidarity: Prioritizing vulnerable families and coastal fisherfolk.
  • Civic Integrity: Upholding transparency, stewardship, and accountability.
  • Environmental Respect: Protecting marine and shoreline ecosystems.
  • Youth Empowerment: Nurturing healthy, purpose-driven future leaders.
Civic Governance Highlight

Private Sector Representative (Fishery Sector) in RDC Caraga

Friendship Goals Society Caraga-Butuan Inc. proudly serves as an accredited Private Sector Representative (PSR) for the Fishery Sector in the Caraga Regional Development Council (RDC).

The RDC is the highest socio-economic planning and policy-coordinating body in the region, bringing together provincial governors, city mayors, regional line agency directors, and accredited private sector leaders.

In this key governance role, FGSCBI actively represents the socio-economic welfare, environmental protection, and livelihood security of Caraga's coastal fishing communities. Our representatives take part in Full Council Meetings—including the 122nd (Second Quarter 2025) Full Council Meeting—ensuring that regional development resolutions directly reflect grassroots realities.
