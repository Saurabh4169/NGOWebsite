Community Action in Caraga

Programs & Projects

Our core initiatives are built on close collaboration with local barangays, regional line agencies, and national institutions to deliver lasting community impact.

National Youth Commission
Partner: National Youth Commission (NYC), Office of the President

1. Youth Anti-Drug Abuse Resistance Education

In November 2021, Friendship Goals Society Caraga-Butuan Inc. formally participated in the nationwide "Search for Outstanding Illegal Drug Abuse Resistance Education Projects for the Youth" (I DARE Youth), conducted by the National Youth Commission (NYC), Office of the President.

The program focuses on preventive drug resistance education, peer guidance, character-building, and positive civic activities designed to guide Butuan youth toward healthy lifestyles, productive leadership, and constructive community contributions.

Milestone: Manila, Philippines (24 November 2021) Youth Leadership
Coastal & Marine Care
Partner: Community Barangays, Local Fisherfolk & Volunteers

2. Environmental & Coastal Clean-ups

Caraga is defined by its rich marine biodiversity, coastlines, and river networks. FGSCBI mobilizes youth, coastal neighborhood residents, and artisanal fisherfolk for regular shoreline sanitation, plastic recovery drives, and eco-stewardship education.

Our clean-up drives target sensitive marine habitats and riverbanks around Butuan Bay, fostering community ownership over environmental protection and emphasizing that clean coasts sustain healthy fishing livelihoods.

Status: Ongoing Volunteer Drives Join as Volunteer
DOLE-Assisted Livelihood
Partner: DOLE Regional Office Caraga (DILP) & Butuan City PESO

3. Community Livelihood: Soap Making & Marketing

A community livelihood training initiative assisted by the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) Regional Office Caraga through the DOLE Integrated Livelihood Program (DILP), in coordination with the Butuan City Public Employment Service Office (PESO).

Launched on May 1, 2025, in Brgy. J.P. Rizal, Butuan City, the project provides community members with practical vocational skills in formulating high-quality household and personal hygiene soaps, product packaging, and local micro-enterprise marketing to help augment sustainable family income.

Started: May 1, 2025 • Brgy. J.P. Rizal, Butuan City Micro-Enterprise
CSO Digital Capacity
Partner: Commission on Human Rights (CHR) & Friedrich Naumann Foundation

4. Human Rights Go Digital: Regional CSO Advocacy

In July 2025, FGSCBI participated in the "Human Rights Go Digital: Regional Digital Advocacy Workshop for Civil Society Organizations (CSOs)" held in Butuan City, co-organized by the Commission on Human Rights (CHR) and the Friedrich Naumann Foundation (FNF) Philippines.

The training strengthened regional civil society advocates in digital campaigning, rights-based communication, online safety, and ethical storytelling, enabling CSOs to effectively champion human dignity and grassroots concerns in online spaces.

Conducted: July 2025 • Butuan City Advocacy & Rights
Regional Policy & Planning
Partner: Caraga Regional Development Council (RDC) & NEDA Caraga

5. Regional Development Council (RDC) Caraga Participation

FGSCBI serves as the accredited Private Sector Representative (PSR) for the Fishery Sector in RDC Caraga. Our leadership actively attended the 122nd (Second Quarter 2025) Full Council Meeting and sectoral deliberations in Butuan City.

By presenting empirical grassroots insights on coastal resources, post-harvest facilities, and artisanal fishing welfare, FGSCBI ensures that regional development investments directly address coastal poverty alleviation and marine conservation.

Role: Fishery Sector PSR • 122nd Council Meeting (July 2025) View Plaque
Social Research Consultation
Partner: Brain Trust Inc. / Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD)

6. Social Protection Research Participation (4Ps Assessment)

FGSCBI participated as a civil society stakeholder in a Focus Group Discussion (FGD) for the national assessment study entitled "Assessment of Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps) and Other Existing Social Protection Programs in the Philippines and their Contribution to Poverty Alleviation", conducted by Brain Trust Inc. on behalf of DSWD.

FGSCBI contributed firsthand perspectives regarding social protection coverage, delivery gaps in coastal fishing villages, and pathways to sustainable community graduation from assistance.
