Community Action in Caraga

Programs & Projects

Our core initiatives are built on close collaboration with local barangays, regional line agencies, and national institutions to deliver lasting community impact.

POSITIVE ADDICTION NYC / Office of the Pres.

1. Youth Anti-Drug Abuse Resistance Education

Partner: National Youth Commission (NYC), Office of the President

Formally recognized in the nationwide "Search for Outstanding Illegal Drug Abuse Resistance Education Projects for the Youth" (I DARE Youth), providing proactive preventive education, peer guidance, character-building, and positive civic activities.

Milestone: Manila, 24 Nov 2021
VIEW MILESTONE DETAILS
COASTAL & MARINE CARE • Routine Drives

2. Environmental & Coastal Clean-ups

Partner: Community Barangays, Local Fisherfolk & Volunteers

Mobilizing Caraga youth, coastal neighborhood residents, and artisanal fisherfolk for regular shoreline sanitation, plastic pollution interception, and mangrove habitat stewardship across Butuan Bay.

Cadence: Ongoing Volunteer Drives
JOIN AS VOLUNTEER
MICRO-ENTERPRISE DOLE Caraga & PESO

3. Community Livelihood: Soap Making & Marketing

Partner: DOLE Regional Office Caraga (DILP) & Butuan PESO

Assisted by the Department of Labor and Employment Integrated Livelihood Program (DILP), delivering hands-on training in formulating household soaps, product packaging, and local micro-enterprise marketing.

Started: May 1, 2025 • Butuan
EXPLORE PROJECT
ADVOCACY & RIGHTS CHR & FNF

4. Human Rights Go Digital: Regional CSO Advocacy

Partner: CHR & Friedrich Naumann Foundation (FNF)

Capacity building for civil society advocates on digital campaigning, rights-based public communication, digital safety protocols, and ethical advocacy across regional online platforms.

Conducted: July 28–29, 2025 • Butuan City
READ HIGHLIGHTS
CIVIC GOVERNANCE RDC & NEDA Caraga

5. Regional Development Council (RDC) Participation

Partner: Caraga RDC & NEDA Regional Office

Serving as the accredited Private Sector Representative (PSR) for the Fishery Sector. Delivering empirical grassroots insights on post-harvest facilities, fuel subsidies, and coastal zoning in RDC Full Council sessions.

Mandate: 122nd Council Session
VIEW COUNCIL BRIEFING
MARINE STEWARDSHIP BFAR Caraga

6. Artisanal Fisherfolk Welfare & Sanctuary Defense

Partner: BFAR Caraga & Coastal Fisherfolk Alliances

Safeguarding artisanal fishing grounds against illegal maritime encroachment, distributing ecological fishing gear, and establishing community-level marine conservation monitoring in Butuan Bay.

Coverage: Caraga Municipal Waters
