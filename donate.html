Frequently Asked Questions

Will I receive an official receipt?

Yes! An official electronic receipt is generated instantly upon payment confirmation and emailed directly to your specified address.

Can overseas or international donors contribute?

Yes. Overseas donors and OFWs can contribute via Visa, Mastercard, or bank wire transfer. Amounts in other currencies are converted automatically at standard bank rates.

Can I donate in-kind supplies or equipment?

Yes. We gratefully accept in-kind donations of coastal clean-up gear, safety vests, and youth educational materials. Please contact our secretariat to coordinate logistics.
