Our Focus Areas

What We Do

Through participatory grassroots initiatives and regional policy engagement, FGSCBI addresses critical social, environmental, and economic needs in Butuan City and the Caraga Region.

YOUTH RESILIENCE

Youth & Anti-Drug Education

Empowering young Caraganons with substance abuse resistance education, peer guidance, and leadership skills. Participated in the Office of the President's National Youth Commission (NYC) I DARE Youth nationwide search.

Learn about Youth Initiatives →
COASTAL CARE

Environmental & Coastal Care

Mobilizing coastal fisherfolk, youth, and neighborhood volunteers for shoreline sanitation, marine habitat protection, and riverbank clean-ups across Butuan Bay and Agusan waterways.

View Coastal Care Efforts →
MICRO-ENTERPRISE

Community Livelihood Support

Delivering hands-on micro-enterprise training such as soap making and marketing in partnership with DOLE Regional Office Caraga (DILP) and the Butuan City PESO to boost household income.

Explore Livelihood Projects →
ADVOCACY & RIGHTS

Human Rights & Digital Advocacy

Strengthening civil society online communication, rights-based storytelling, and community advocacy through workshops organized by the Commission on Human Rights and Friedrich Naumann Foundation.

Read Advocacy Work →
CIVIC GOVERNANCE

Regional Development Governance

Serving as the accredited Private Sector Representative (PSR) for the Fishery Sector in the Caraga Regional Development Council (RDC), bringing coastal concerns into regional planning.

Discover RDC Representation →
SOCIAL RESEARCH

Social Protection & Research

Contributing civil society insights to research consultations and Focus Group Discussions assessing national social protection programs like the DSWD Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps).

Learn About Social Studies →
